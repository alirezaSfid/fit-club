# training-plan-final.md

**Version 4.0 — 7 November 2026.** The v3 gym structure is unchanged (full body A/B/C, 3×/week, Sportvision). This version changes four things:

- The post-gym shake and the "trained today" toggle are gone; dinner is the post-workout meal.
- Alireza takes a load step this week.
- Walking becomes a daily, tracked part of the plan, aimed at visceral fat.
- There is now a clear rule for when running is allowed.

## Why the structure stays

FitClub Month 2 was a body-part split: ~100 working sets a week, drop sets, and sessions of 90–105 minutes against a one-hour limit. v3 replaced it with full body 3×/week: ~56 sets, every muscle trained 2–3× a week, ~55 min per session, machines first. Alireza's Matrix analysis shows muscle going up, so the programme is working. The weak point is the hours outside the gym, not the gym itself.

## Structure

- **Full body 3×/week.** One rest day between sessions where possible. Choose the days each morning and keep the **A → B → C** order.
- **Sessions are in the evening.** The 16:30 tea snack is the pre-workout snack. The 19:30 dinner straight after is the post-workout meal. There is **no shake and no toggle any more**: what matters is total daily protein, not eating within minutes of the session.
- **Warm-up:** 4 min easy bike, then hip and shoulder circles (5 min in total).
- **Rest between sets:** compound exercises 90–120 s, isolation exercises 60–75 s.

## Daily movement — the visceral-fat lever

Exercise reduces visceral fat even when the scale doesn't move. Moderate aerobic work is the strongest form. So walking is treated as training, not as a bonus.

| | Now | By 1 Dec | By mid-Jan |
|---|---|---|---|
| Steps, daily average (wear the watch every day) | ~6,000 | **8,000** | **10,000** |
| Daylight walk | — | 20–30 min every day, in the morning or at lunch | same |
| Long walk together | — | Sunday, 60 min | same |

Total moderate activity: cardio finishers (3 × 12–20 min) plus daily walks comes to about **250+ min a week**. That is the level linked to clinically meaningful weight loss and better weight maintenance.

## Running — the rule

- **Ana:** if no knee, hip or ankle hurts, she can start **walk–run twice a week on non-gym days**.
  - Week 1: 6 × (1 min easy jog + 2 min walk).
  - Each week, add 30 s to every jog and keep the walks at 2 min.
  - Aim to reach 20–25 min of continuous easy running in about 8–10 weeks.
  - Run on soft ground or a treadmill, in proper running shoes. Any joint pain that lasts into the next day means repeating the previous week.
- **Alireza:** **not yet.** At his current weight, every running stride puts a load of several times his body weight through the knees. Use the incline walk and the bike instead.
  - He starts the same walk–run plan, beginning at 1 min jog + 3 min walk, once **both** of these are true: (1) he is under ~110 kg, and (2) a 45-min brisk walk leaves no joint pain the next day.

## Load and progression

- Choose a load where **set 1 finishes with 3 reps in reserve** (RIR). Sets 2–3 then land at 1–2 RIR.
- **Log weight × reps for every set** in the Matrix app or Apple Health.
- **Alireza — the week of 9 Nov is a load week.** On every exercise where your last session hit the top of the rep range in all sets, add the smallest increment and drop back to the bottom of the range. Everything else stays where it is.
- After that the cycle runs again, changing one lever at a time:
  - Weeks 2–4 (16 Nov – 6 Dec): reps only.
  - Week 5 (7 Dec): load.
  - Then repeat.
- **Cardio grows by duration only:** add 2 min every second week until the finisher is 20 min. From then on, add steps instead.
- **Autoregulation:** if reps drop on the same exercise two sessions in a row, cut one set from each isolation exercise for a week and keep the load the same. Don't add food.
