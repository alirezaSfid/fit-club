## Rules that make it stick

**Saturday social dinner.** One plate with meat or fish in it, plus one dessert *or* drinks. Drinks are every other week: Alireza 2, Ana 1, and they come out of the same budget. Breakfast and lunch on Saturday are eaten as planned; don't "save up" for dinner. The next meal goes back to plan, with no compensation.

**A party or friends on another day.** That event becomes the week's social dinner. Saturday dinner then becomes **Plan B: omelet-e gojeh, salad and bread from what's in the house**, which fits inside the budget. Before going out, eat the tea snack; at the event, eat protein and vegetables first; take one plate and one dessert, and drink water between drinks.

**Travel: no diet, five habits.**
1. Protein at every meal.
2. Half the plate vegetables or salad, eaten first.
3. One plate at a buffet and one dessert a day.
4. Walk 10,000+ steps.
5. Alternate alcohol with water.

Back home, weigh the next morning and go straight back to the plan that day, with no compensation. Ignore the jump on the scale for 7–10 days; it is mostly water.

**Office day (Alireza).** Pack that day's breakfast jar and lunch box. If you eat in the canteen instead: protein + vegetables + one starch, nothing fried, water.

**Hunger.** Alireza before dinner: drink 500 ml water and eat the salad first, and never skip the tea snack. Ana before sleep: the TV snack is hers to keep; cucumber and tomato are free at any time.

**Weighing.** Weigh every morning in Apple Health. On Sunday, send the **7-day average**: it smooths out water, and Ana's cycle (about 0.5 kg around her period). Do a Matrix body analysis every 4 weeks for visceral fat.

**Sleep and light.** Get up at the same time every day. Take a 20–30 min daylight walk in the morning or at lunch. No screens for 30 min before bed. From October to March, take vitamin D 10 µg daily; this is Dutch advice for anyone who is outdoors little in daylight.

## Sunday cook-up (about 2.5 h, three pots at once)

1. **Chelo goosht**: tonight's dinner. Start it first; it needs 1.5–2 h.
2. **Ash**: Monday's lunch. Fridge.
3. **Ghormeh sabzi**: Wednesday's dinner. Fridge, or freeze and thaw it on Tuesday night.
4. **Portion the chicken** for Monday's zereshk polo (both portions: Monday dinner and Tuesday lunch).
5. On Thursday night, marinate the Friday jujeh (bought on Tuesday). Overnight oats are made the night before Tuesday and Thursday.
