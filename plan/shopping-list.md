# shopping-list.md

**Version 4.0 — 7 November 2026.** Derived from diet-plan-final.md v4.0, both clients summed, every gram computed by `fitclub/shop.py` from `data/plan.py` (run `python build.py`). Nothing is typed by hand.

## How to read it

- **Two trips.** Saturday covers Sat · Sun · Mon · Tue breakfast and lunch, plus the ghormeh sabzi you cook on Sunday for Wednesday. Tuesday covers Tue tea and dinner through Friday.
- **Store stickers:** `[Lidl]`, `[AH]`, `[Jumbo]`, `[AH Terra]` mark where the item is clearly better or only sold. No sticker means any store.
- **Pantry items** (rice, oats, nuts, oil, whey…) are listed separately with how long each pack lasts. Add one to whichever trip you're on when it runs low.
- **Persian pantry:** once a month at an Iranian/Turkish shop. There is no supermarket alternative.
- **Bio worth it:** salad, spinach, pepper, fresh herbs, berries, eggs, oats.

## First week only (10 Nov)

The 7 Nov Saturday shop used the v3.5 list, so on **Tuesday 10 Nov** add three things to the Tuesday list:

1. **The ghormeh sabzi items:** runderpoulet 225 g, 1 tin of kidney beans, 270 g herbs. Cook it on Tuesday afternoon (it needs about 2 h) for Wednesday's dinner.
2. **1 L milk** for Wednesday's sauce and Thursday's oats.
3. **Dried fenugreek and limoo amani** for the ghormeh, if you have none. If there's no time for the Iranian/Turkish shop, use ½ lime and skip the fenugreek this once.

The barberries aren't needed until Monday 16 Nov.
