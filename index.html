How to load it

  • Pick a weight where set 1 finishes with 3 reps left in the tank. Sets 2–3 will land at 1–2 left.
  • If set 1 is already a grind, the weight is too heavy. You are in a deficit — not the day for heroics.
  • Log weight × reps for every set in Apple Health or the Matrix app — the log is the whole progression system.
  • Weeks 1–3 add reps only. Week 4 add weight only, smallest increment, back to the bottom of the rep range. Weeks 5–7 reps again. One lever at a time.
  • Cardio grows by duration only — +2 min every second week until 20 min.
  • If reps drop on the same exercise two sessions running, drop one set from each isolation for a week and hold the weight — do not add food.

Why four meals and not seven

Meal frequency has no effect on fat loss, muscle retention or appetite once calories and protein are held equal. What matters is 30–40 g of protein per sitting, which four meals plus the +1 does comfortably.

The free meal
