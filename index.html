How to load it

  • Pick a weight where set 1 finishes with 3 reps left in the tank. Sets 2–3 will land at 1–2 left.
  • If set 1 is already a grind, the weight is too heavy. You are in a deficit — that is not the day for heroics.
  • Log weight × reps for every set. Not optional. The log is the whole progression system.
  • Weeks 1–3 add reps only, weight fixed. Week 4 add weight only, smallest increment, back to the bottom of the rep range. Weeks 5–7 reps again. One lever at a time.
  • Cardio grows by duration only — add 2 min every second week until you reach 20 min. Never raise time and intensity together.
  • If reps drop on the same exercise two sessions running, that is fatigue. Drop one set from each isolation for a week and hold the weight — do not add food.

Every meal

  • Protein → vegetables → starch, physically in that order on the plate.
  • 500 ml water before every meal. 20 minutes minimum. No screens.
  • Big salad before dinner, maximum 1 tbsp olive oil — and that tablespoon comes out of the day's oil, it does not sit on top. Dressed heavily, a starter salad makes people eat more, not less.
  • One system: everything weighed raw or dry before cooking. No cooked weights, no spoons.
  • Never skip a meal to save calories — that is what produces the evening binge.
  • Multivitamin in the evening. Sleep 7–8 h. Weigh daily, trust only the 7-day average.

Why there is no gym-day bonus

Not because training burns nothing — a session plus its finisher is a real 250–350 kcal. It is because you pick your gym days each morning. A bonus has to be eaten before you know whether you trained, so on every skipped day it becomes a surplus. That is precisely what stalled Ana on the old plan.

Nothing was taken away — the week is within 150 kcal of v2. The gym days came down about 150 and the rest days went up about 75. Fat loss follows the weekly total, not the daily shape.

Fix your gym days to Mon/Wed/Fri and the bonus becomes safe — I would then move ~150 kcal off each rest day onto each gym day, same weekly total. Say the word and it is a ten-minute change.

Why four meals and not seven

Meal frequency has no effect on fat loss, muscle retention or appetite once calories and protein are held equal — and more meals does not mean less hunger. What matters is getting 30–40 g of protein per sitting, which four meals plus the shake does comfortably. Seven meals would only have split the same protein into doses too small to count.

The free meal

One per week, Saturday lunch. It is budgeted at 700 kcal, not unlimited — an 1,800 kcal restaurant meal wipes out almost a fifth of the week's deficit on its own. Pick something with meat in it; protein runs low that day otherwise. One per week, never two.

Two things AH and Lidl genuinely do not stock: limoo amani (dried limes, for ghormeh and gheymeh) and dried fenugreek (shanbalileh, for the sabzi). Both are shelf-stable for a year, so one bag of each covers twelve months. Until then, use the juice and zest of half a fresh lime per pot in place of the dried limes — it is sharper and less earthy, but it works. The sabzi without fenugreek is still good; it just is not textbook.

Where this is going

At 2,200 kcal held steady, the deficit shrinks on its own as you get lighter — roughly 25 kcal of daily burn per kg lost. Projected: 0.79 kg/wk now → 0.66 at 115 kg → 0.54 at 110 kg → 0.43 at 105 kg. That is when the first calorie cut is due, not before. Around 105 kg, take 150–200 kcal off and hold for four weeks.

The real risk

Appetite rises about 100 kcal/day for every kg lost — roughly four times faster than metabolism falls. The thing that stalls people is not a broken metabolism, it is intake quietly drifting up. That is why the food gets weighed, and why untracked cooking oil is capped at 2 tbsp.
